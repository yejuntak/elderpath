The State of US Nursing Home Safety, 2026

We analyzed all 14,700 Medicare-certified nursing homes in the latest federal dataset. 19.6% are rated 1 star — the lowest possible — and 40.1% sit in the bottom two tiers. 1,482 facilities (1 in 10) carry an active CMS abuse flag, and facilities have paid $462 million in federal fines.

The abuse flag is not a technicality: CMS attaches it only when inspectors cite a facility for abuse that led to harm within the past year, or potential harm in each of the last two years. One in ten certified facilities in America currently carries it.

Texas spotlight: 7th-worst state, $59.9M in fines

Texas has more certified nursing homes than any other state (1,176). 24.2% are rated 1 star, 47.9% sit in the bottom two tiers, and 70.2% have been fined — the data point that surprised us most, against a 45.1% national rate.

Why is assisted living even harder to research than nursing homes?

Everything above exists because nursing homes are federally certified — CMS publishes one national dataset anyone can download. Assisted living has no federal equivalent. Its inspection records are scattered across 50 separate state agencies, mostly as PDFs, with no common rating system — while referral platforms that families turn to are paid by the facilities they recommend. A 2024 Washington Post analysis found more than a third of facilities the largest referral site gave "Best of Senior Living" awards had been cited for neglect or substandard care, and the Senate Aging Committee opened an investigation that June.

Methodology & sources

All statistics were computed from the CMS Provider Information dataset (Nursing homes including rehab services; May 2026 monthly release, published May 27, 2026; 14,700 facilities), retrieved and analyzed June 10, 2026. Ratings are the CMS Five-Star Quality Rating System's Overall Rating. The abuse measure is the CMS abuse icon. Fines are the "Total Amount of Fines in Dollars" field on each facility's current record. State table excludes states and territories with fewer than 20 certified facilities. Analysis code and aggregates available on request.
