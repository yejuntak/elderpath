36 months of state licensing inspections and citations, weighted by severity and whether corrections were verified. Serious-harm citations cap the grade at C.

Staff-to-resident ratios and 12-month turnover versus state averages — the strongest day-to-day predictors of care quality.

Gap between advertised "starting" price and what resident families actually pay. Communities with bait pricing lose points.

How we're paid

Our business model, in plain English.

You shouldn't have to guess who a "free" service really works for. Here's the whole model:

✓What we do

  • Communities pay a flat placement fee when a family moves in — same fee for every community, never a percentage of rent
  • Families can choose a $299 independent-advisor tier: we refund any facility fee and work only for you
  • Every report card cites its public sources so you can check our work

✕What we never do

  • Sell or share your contact information with facilities, marketers, or anyone else
  • Let a community pay for a better grade, a higher ranking, or a spot on your shortlist
  • Hide a community because it doesn't pay us — non-paying communities appear with full report cards
