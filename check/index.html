Where does this data come from?

Every result is drawn unaltered from the CMS Provider Information dataset (May 2026 monthly release) — the federal government's official record for all Medicare- and Medicaid-certified nursing homes. The star rating is CMS's Five-Star Quality Rating; the abuse flag is CMS's own abuse icon; fines are federal enforcement totals on the facility's current record.

Context from our analysis of the full dataset: 19.6% of US nursing homes are rated 1 star, 1,482 carry an active abuse flag, and facilities have paid $462M in federal fines. See The State of US Nursing Home Safety, 2026 — every state ranked.

A note on assisted living: this lookup covers nursing homes, because they're the only senior-care setting with a federal dataset. Assisted-living records are scattered across 50 state agencies — unifying them into the same kind of report card is what ElderPath is being built to do.
