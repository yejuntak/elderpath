Before you sign anything — the contract checklist
  1. The all-in monthly number, in writing. Base rate + care-level charges + medication management + incontinence supplies + laundry. Ask which can change and with how much notice.
  2. Care-level reassessment. Who decides when care needs (and fees) increase, how often, and can you get an independent assessment?
  3. Discharge and eviction terms. Under what conditions can they ask your parent to leave? Hospital stays — is the bed held, at what cost?
  4. Medicaid policy. If savings run out, can your parent stay on Medicaid? Get it in writing — "Medicaid-pending" policies differ wildly.
  5. Deposits and refunds. Community fee refundable? Pro-rated month on move-out or death? (Families get burned here constantly.)
  6. Arbitration clause. Many contracts waive your right to sue. You can often decline this clause and still be admitted — ask.
  7. Responsible-party language. Never sign as personally financially responsible — sign as agent/POA only.
  8. Staffing commitments. Anything verbal ("an RN is always in the building") — ask them to write it in.
  9. Personal needs and extras. Salon, transport to appointments, activities — included or billed?
  10. Termination by you. Notice period and any penalty to move out if it isn't working.
